# Etsy listing skeleton

**Title:** [benefit] + [product type] + [format]

**Tags (13):** primary keyword, audience, format, use-case, ...

**Description structure:**
1. Hook (what problem you solve)
2. What's included (bullets)
3. Who it's for
4. How to use (3 steps)
5. FAQ / refunds (digital: no refund after download — be clear)
